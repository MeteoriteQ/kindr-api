About Kindr

Built to make giving feel simple, safe, and human.

Kindr helps people raise money for urgent needs, meaningful dreams, and community causes with clear campaign pages and donor-friendly tools.

Our impact

10k+fundraisers supported
25Cr+raised across causes
24/7campaign assistance

We believe help should reach people faster.

Medical emergencies, education costs, and personal crises can arrive without warning. Kindr gives campaign organizers a clear way to explain their need and invite trusted support.

Our goal is to make fundraising approachable for first-time users while keeping donors informed and confident about where their contribution goes.

From campaign creation to updates and sharing, every interaction is designed to be simple, respectful, and transparent.
