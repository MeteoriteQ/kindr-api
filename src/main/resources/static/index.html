FAQ

Quick answers before you begin.

A short set of common questions for donors and fundraisers getting started on kindr.

How do I start a fundraiser?

Click Start a fundraiser, choose a category, add your goal and story, then publish and share the campaign link with supporters.

Can donors browse by category?

Yes. Donors can explore medical, education, non-profit, animal, and emergency campaigns from the donate page.

What makes a campaign trustworthy?

A clear story, accurate goal, helpful documents, and regular updates help donors understand the need and feel confident giving.

Can I update my fundraiser after publishing?

Yes. Campaign updates are useful for sharing progress, thanking donors, and explaining how the funds are helping.
